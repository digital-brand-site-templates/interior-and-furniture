Living rooms that work for everyday life and for guests.

We plan the room around light, conversation and the screen — never the other way round — then draw, cost and deliver every piece of it, from the joinery to the last cushion.

Included

What is included

  • Measured survey

    Every wall, window and socket drawn to scale before we design.

  • Layout options

    Two or three furniture plans, each tested against how you use the room.

  • Lighting plan

    Ceiling, wall and lamp lighting on separate circuits, set for day and evening.

  • Colour and materials

    A sample board of paint, fabric and finishes, seen in your own light.

  • Furniture sourcing

    Trade prices on everything we specify, ordered and tracked by us.

  • Delivery on site

    One project manager, weekly updates and a snag list closed before hand-over.

Process

How we work

  1. Consultation

    A free 45-minute conversation at your home, in the studio or on video.

  2. Concept

    Mood, layout options and a first cost range within two weeks.

  3. Design and costing

    Final drawings, a lighting plan and a fixed quote for every item.

  4. Delivery

    We order, schedule the trades and hand over a finished room.

Questions

Good to know

How long does a living-room project take?

Most take eight to twelve weeks from the first consultation to hand-over; bespoke joinery adds three to four weeks.

Can you work with furniture we already own?

Yes — we survey what you have first and design around the pieces you want to keep.

Do you only work in Richmond?

We work across Virginia in person and further afield with video meetings and a local site team.

What does it cost?

Design starts at $2,400 for a single room; see the pricing page for the full-design and turnkey packages.

Residential care project in Paris

A 1920s townhouse turned into a care residence for 32 guests. The brief: rooms that feel like a family home, not a clinic — with every handrail, light level and door width meeting the standards a care home needs.

Client
Clairval Care Group
Location
Paris, France
Year
2025
Area
1,850 m²
Scope
Interior architecture, furniture, wayfinding
Duration
14 months
01

The brief

Keep the character of the original house — the stone walls, the oak floors, the tall windows — while making every room safe, easy to clean and easy to find your way around.

02

Our approach

Colour-coded floors for wayfinding, warm 2700K lighting that never glares, and furniture with arms and firm seats chosen with the residents themselves at two tasting days.

03

The result

Occupancy reached 100% within three months of opening, and the family survey rated the lounges and dining room 4.8 out of 5 for feeling like home.

Interior-Vale is an interior architecture studio in Richmond, Virginia. Since 2009 we have planned and finished more than 240 homes, offices and hospitality spaces — from a single reading corner to a four-storey townhouse.

Every project starts with how you use a room, not with a mood board. We measure, draw and cost each option before anything is ordered, then manage the trades on site so the finished space looks exactly like the plan you signed off.
